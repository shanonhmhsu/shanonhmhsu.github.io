I am often looking to hire full/part-time RAs. An advertisement for open positions as of September 2026 is [here](https://econ.ntu.edu.tw/archives/18559).

If you are interested, please [email](mailto:shmhsu@ntu.edu.tw) me a single PDF containing:

1. Cover letter (brief) outlining: (i) your background and career goals, (ii) your experience in conducting or assisting with research projects, and (iii) your experience and familiarity with any programming languages, with short examples of code you’ve written.
2. CV.
3. Transcript.
4. Writing sample (optional): a thesis, term paper, or other research output you have written yourself.

Please also feel free to [email](mailto:shmhsu@ntu.edu.tw) me if you would like to learn more about the RA positions.
